Why Orlando Homes Need Surge Protection
  • Central Florida gets more lightning than anywhere else in the country. Most surge damage doesn't come from a direct hit — it travels in through the utility line.
  • Your AC is the expensive one. Replacing a surge-damaged compressor or control board runs into the thousands. The protector costs a fraction of one repair.
  • Most surges start inside the house. Every time a large motor cycles — AC, pool pump, refrigerator — it sends a spike down the line. They add up and shorten the life of your electronics.
  • Power strips aren't the same thing. They only protect what's plugged into them, and most are rated far below what a real surge carries.

How the Installation Works

  1. We look at your panel first. Free assessment, on site or from photos you send us. We confirm what your panel can take before quoting anything.
  2. You get the price up front. Device, labor and warranty, itemized. If we find something unusual, you hear about it before we start.
  3. We install and test. About two hours for a standard job. Power goes off briefly while we work inside the panel.
  4. You keep the paperwork. Device details and warranty documentation, in case you ever need to make a claim.
