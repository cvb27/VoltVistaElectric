Why You Need Surge Protection

  • Lightning strikes, utility switching, and power surges can damage electronics and appliances.
  • A single surge can cost thousands in repairs to HVAC, water heaters, washers and smart home devices.
  • Whole-home surge protection stops surges at the panel — before they reach your equipment.
  • Florida law (NEC 2020) recommends surge protection for new residential installations.

Our Surge Protector Installation Process

  1. Free on-site assessment of your panel and protection needs.
  2. Detailed estimate with device type, installation labor and warranty details.
  3. Professional installation at your main electrical panel.
  4. Final inspection and documentation for insurance and warranty claims.

Types of Surge Protection We Install

  • Whole-Home Type 2 Surge Protectors: Panel-mounted protection for your entire home.
  • Point-of-Use Surge Protectors: Targeted protection for sensitive equipment (media centers, home offices).
  • Combination Systems: Multi-layer protection for maximum safety.
