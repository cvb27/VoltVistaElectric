Why Charge at Home

  • Lower cost per mile. Charging on your home rate is typically far cheaper than public fast chargers.
  • No more detours. Skip the trip to a charging station and the wait for an open stall.
  • Full battery every morning. Plug in at night, leave with your full range every day.
  • Better for your battery. Slower overnight charging is gentler on the pack than repeated fast charging.
  • Adds appeal to your home. A ready-to-use charging circuit is a feature buyers and renters look for.

Level 1 vs Level 2 — Which One Do You Need?

The right choice depends on how far you drive each day and how fast you want to recover range.

  • Level 1 (120V). Uses a standard outlet. Adds roughly 3–5 miles of range per hour. Fine for plug-in hybrids or short daily commutes.
  • Level 2 (240V). Runs on a dedicated circuit and charges several times faster — most EVs go from low to full overnight. This is what the majority of homeowners end up installing.
